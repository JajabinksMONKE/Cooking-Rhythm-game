Lunar New Year Pixel Pack - version 1.02
============================================================

78 pixel art sprites for celebrating Chinese New Year / Lunar New Year
in your 2D game: lanterns, couplets, door gods, festive food, red envelopes,
temple props, buildings and fireworks. Made for a 64 x 64 px tile grid.

QUICK TIPS
  - Use nearest-neighbour (point) filtering and whole-number scaling (x2, x3)
    to keep the pixels sharp.
  - Objects stand on the bottom of their canvas: use a bottom-centre pivot.
  - Shadows and outlines are already included.
  - Pairs such as stone lions and door gods are meant to be mirrored.
